DRIVING CONTROLS

DRIVETRAIN · RANGE · LOCKS

Manual: Q down, E up, B neutral. Sequence R → N → 1 → …; clutch is automatic. On R, W/↑ accelerates and S/↓ brakes. Stop before changing direction; downshifts protect against over-revving.

A/M controls gear shifting. HI/LO selects the transfer-case range. Crawl mode adds reduction and differential locks. Enter crawl or change locks below 8 km/h. Keys 1–4 select modes; Z/X toggle front/rear locks.
